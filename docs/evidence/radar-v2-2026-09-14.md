## Evidence boundary

The screenshots use deterministic QA rows and are interface evidence, not live performance claims. A fresh five-block live smoke cycle reached Robinhood Chain head `62,896,831`, saved both factory and market cursors at that head, and reported zero lag. Reopening the same SQLite database advanced both cursors to `62,897,079`, which verifies persisted restart recovery and overlap replay. No launches or trades occurred inside that narrow sample, so it verifies transport, cursor, block-hash, and restart behavior rather than feed completeness.

The public RPC rejected the original archival `eth_getCode` deployment search below its retained history (`metadata is not found`). Bootstrap now verifies factory bytecode at the current head and uses an optional `MEERKAT_RADAR_START_BLOCK` floor; it does not require archive state. Pair-token PnL remains in pair units; native pairs display ETH; missing executable quotes and material transfer gaps remain `UNKNOWN`.
